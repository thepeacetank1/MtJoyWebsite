Meet Dr. Randolph

A practice shaped around relationship.

Dr. Jeannine Randolph founded Mt. Joy Medicine with a straightforward idea: good care depends on good listening. Her approach makes room for the details that can be missed when medicine moves too quickly.

As the clinic’s sole physician, Dr. Randolph remains directly involved in each patient’s care. That continuity supports clearer conversations, more thoughtful decisions, and a stronger understanding of each person’s health over time.
