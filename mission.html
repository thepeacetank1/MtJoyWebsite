Our mission

Make thoughtful medicine feel personal again.

Mount Joy Medicine exists to create a steady, trusting relationship between one physician and the patients she serves.

We believe people deserve care that listens closely, explains clearly, and considers the full context of their lives.

Guiding principles

What the practice is built to protect.

Time to listen

Useful care starts with hearing the whole concern—not just the first symptom named.

Continuity that matters

A long-term physician relationship adds context to every new question and decision.

Clarity without distance

Medical information should be explained plainly, respectfully, and without unnecessary barriers.

Decisions made together

Care plans should reflect clinical evidence alongside each patient’s values and circumstances.

The promise

Small by design.

Mount Joy Medicine is intentionally a single-physician clinic. Its size supports a direct relationship with Dr. Randolph and keeps the work of care close to the person receiving it.
